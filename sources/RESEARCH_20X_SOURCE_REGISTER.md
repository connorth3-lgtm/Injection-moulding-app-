# MouldMaster 20-Pass Research Source Register

Status date: 2026-08-24

Purpose: document the twenty distinct research passes used for `reference-20x-extension.js`. The extension converts research into learning-oriented mechanisms, evidence prompts and diagnostic concepts. It deliberately does **not** copy paper-specific optimum machine settings into general production guidance.

## Evidence-use rules

1. A published experiment demonstrates behaviour under its own material, mould, machine and test conditions; it does not create a universal machine recipe.
2. Resin-supplier grade data and validated moisture/drying requirements control grade-specific material handling.
3. Machine, hot-runner, robot, mould and auxiliary supplier documentation controls equipment-specific limits and maintenance methods.
4. Safety guidance is governed by current applicable law, regulator guidance and machinery standards, not by experimental optimisation literature.
5. Simulation and machine-learning results require local experimental validation before production acceptance or automated quality disposition.
6. Environmental comparisons require an explicit functional unit, system boundary, allocation method and data assumptions.
7. Recycled-material qualification must retain feedstock and reprocessing-history traceability; MFR/MVR alone is not a complete property or rheology specification.
8. Vision, sensor, anomaly and predictive-maintenance models require measurement-system controls, ground truth and drift monitoring.
9. Overmoulded/interface strength is geometry, material, surface, temperature and loading-mode dependent; one coupon value is not universal.
10. Recheck publication, standard and supplier status before a major formal release or accreditation submission.
