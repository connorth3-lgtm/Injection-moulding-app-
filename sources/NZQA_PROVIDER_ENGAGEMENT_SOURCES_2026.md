## Consent and moderation transition

- Injection Moulding domain: https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/framework/explore/domain-full.do?frameworkId=75775
- NZQA CMR 0014 v15 review: https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nqfdocs/summaries/2025/aug25/revsumaug25-01.pdf
- MAEISB consent-to-assess application: https://maeisb.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Consent-to-assess-application-form-MaE-ISB-Jun-2026-V2.pdf
- Current evidence state: selected Injection Moulding standards display CMR 13 on the live domain page; NZQA's 2025 review makes CMR 0014 v15 the core Hanga-Aro-Rau framework for new consent applications and MAEISB's current application material directs Manufacturing and Engineering applicants to CMR 0014.
- MAEISB's current consent-to-assess form directs providers to `moderation@maeisb.nz` and states that NZQA makes the final consent-to-assess decision.
- Required action: obtain current MAEISB/NZQA confirmation of the applicable combination before an actual consent application or recognised assessment design.
