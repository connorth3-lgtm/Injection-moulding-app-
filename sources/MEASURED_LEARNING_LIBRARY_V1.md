# Measured Learning Library v1

## Scope

MouldMaster's Measured Learning Library has a governed curriculum target of **70 cases**. The catalogue is not a claim that 70 measured learner cases already exist. A catalogue row becomes learner-visible only after an exact source/window binding is reviewed, built into a compact case asset and passes `qa_measured_learning_library.py`.

The current catalogue is restricted to source families whose execution-ledger state begins with `accepted-profiled`. Partially accepted, rights-blocked, mirror-rights-blocked, embargoed, request-only, confidential and retrieved-not-accepted sources cannot be promoted merely to fill the 70-case target.

## Evidence tiers

- **Synthetic** — controlled teaching data generated by MouldMaster. The scenario cause can be known because the teaching case created it.
- **Measured** — real measured experimental or industrial behaviour. The default claim scope is observation only; bounded associations may be taught when the source supports them. Real data alone do not establish production root cause.
- **Site validated** — measured production evidence paired with an independently investigated production finding. This tier is reserved for evidence satisfying the authorised real-site validation contract; it is not granted by the public measured library.

`validated_mechanism` is prohibited for ordinary public measured promotion.

## Learner flow

Every promoted case follows:

**Observe → Quantify → Compare → Investigate → Explain → Evidence boundary**

The evidence-boundary section must state both what the measurements support and what they do not establish. Advanced cases deliberately include insufficient-evidence decisions so that learners practise avoiding premature diagnosis.

## 70-case curriculum balance

The catalogue is locked to:

- 20 foundation cases;
- 30 intermediate cases;
- 20 advanced cases;
- at least 10 insufficient-evidence cases;
- at least 10 multi-signal cases;
- at least 10 quality/outcome cases;
- at least 10 time-series interpretation cases;
- at least 8 material/physical-behaviour cases;
- no source family contributing more than 14 of the 70 cases.

The catalogue is weighted toward richer measured sources rather than forcing equal family coverage.

## Promotion contract

A reviewed binding package must provide:

1. catalogue case ID and exact eligible source family;
2. exact source SHA-256 fingerprint;
3. exact extraction/window description;
4. resolved signal/outcome identity, semantics and engineering units;
5. compact deterministic learner representation with original point count retained;
6. versioned calculated features where used;
7. engineering-reviewed observations with explicit evidence links;
8. learner Observe/Investigate/Explain content;
9. supported conclusions;
10. unsupported conclusions;
11. explicit limitations;
12. engineering evidence review metadata.

`tools/build_measured_learning_case.py` computes a deterministic source-window fingerprint and final case fingerprint. `qa_measured_learning_library.py` independently checks the promoted asset.

## Numerical representation

Raw third-party source files and millions of source rows are not bundled into the learner PWA. A promoted signal normally contains no more than 600 displayed points and records:

- original point count;
- displayed x/y values;
- deterministic reduction method;
- signal semantic;
- unit;
- exact source/window fingerprint.

Reduction must preserve the teaching evidence. A smaller payload is not a justification for hiding a transient or changing the engineering interpretation.

## Causal-language boundary

Unless independently established evidence qualifies for the site-validated tier, learner-visible measured cases must avoid unsupported formulations such as "caused by", "root cause was", "proves that", "confirmed fault" or "resulted from".

Preferred wording distinguishes observation from hypothesis, for example:

- "variation increased";
- "the signals moved together in this interval";
- "this is consistent with...";
- "this would warrant investigating...";
- "the measurements do not establish...";
- "insufficient evidence to distinguish...".

## Source rights

Source licence/access restrictions remain attached to the source family. A research/education-only or noncommercial source is not widened by conversion into a MouldMaster case. Reviewers must confirm that the compact learner representation is permitted by the recorded source terms before promotion.

## Relationship to the real-site pilot

The public Measured Learning Library does **not** close the real-site validation requirement. The strongest bridge remains:

**measurement → MouldMaster ranked mechanism → independent site investigation → corrective action → recovery evidence**.

A future site-validated case should use the same learner renderer and provenance structure, with an additional validation block linking MouldMaster's ranked mechanism to the independent production finding. Public measured associations must not be relabelled as that evidence.
