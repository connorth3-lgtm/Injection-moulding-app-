# MouldMaster — Mechanism Validation Queue

Reviewed: 2026-08-29

This queue turns the evidence-coverage audit into executable work. It does **not** claim that formal mechanism promotion completes real-production validation. A public or site dataset should only be used where its actual fields, material/tool context and physical outcomes match the relationship being tested.

Promotion means only that the bounded mechanism-level educational relationship has passed the repository evidence gate. It does not create a universal setpoint, acceptance limit, guaranteed root cause, production-change authority, manufacturer approval or site validation.

## Lane A — executable measured-data validation

### Recyclate and process variability

**Best target:** RWTH post-consumer-recycled material process data.

The study/dataset pair already contributes Tier A literature evidence, but the repository automation path is still blocked by an interstitial rather than exact archive bytes. Keep it non-counting as an executed benchmark until the lawful version-pinned source bytes are retrieved, fingerprinted and profiled.

When accessible, preserve screw-antechamber pressure, cavity pressure, controller output, screw velocity/volume, material identity and part mass. Do not infer a universal PCR correction factor or controller setting.

### Process dynamics and quality linkage

Use the already executed Mendeley, scatimdata, LoKI, ProBayes, iGuzzini, OpenMMS and other accepted packages to verify relationships only where their actual schema supports them. Prefer cavity or waveform evidence over scalar settings where available, preserve actual-versus-command semantics, and keep cycle/part/production-record units distinct.

### Sensor fusion and cavity/process identity

Use OpenMMS, FORinFPRO-HIMD, LoKI, scatimdata and suitable future datasets to test sensor-location meaning, timestamp alignment, modality disagreement and cavity identity. A signal from one location or modality must not be presented as interchangeable with another without calibration evidence.

### Energy and machine state

The accepted Zenodo plant-energy package provides a large real measured electrical stream. Use it for machine-state/energy education only within its direct physical channels and timestamp/machine identity. It does not establish mould-cavity root causes or universal energy limits.

### Upstream process variation and downstream quality

Use the cross-process-chain archive to teach traceability from moulding to downstream assembly while keeping association separate from proven upstream causation. Downstream screw-driving observations remain excluded from injection-cycle counts.

## Lane B — evidence redundancy and scope strengthening

All 12 priority mechanisms are formally promoted, so further literature work should no longer chase status labels. It should strengthen independence, expand material/tool/machine diversity and find contradictory or limiting evidence.

High-value additions are:

- third and fourth independent retained-fibre-length programmes across GF/PP, GF/PA and direct-fibre-feeding architectures;
- multicavity evidence across cold-runner, hot-runner, naturally balanced and rheologically imbalanced tools;
- hot-runner heater-zone actual temperature/current/duty and valve-actuation actuals, not displayed setpoints alone;
- LSR cure evidence across formulations, optical/medical geometries and different cure-state measurements;
- gas-, water- and projectile-assisted studies retained as separate process families;
- point-of-use moisture/water-content measurements across hygroscopic grades with mechanical, rheological and appearance outcomes;
- recycled-feedstock studies with traceable source/lot, repeated recycling, contamination/degradation and physical outcomes;
- micro/nano replication studies with independent metrology and demoulding/release evidence;
- injection-compression optical studies with birefringence, wavefront/optical and dimensional measurements.

A new study should only increase the accepted primary-measured count if it is peer-reviewed, publisher-verified, genuinely measured and represents a unique experiment. Review papers, simulations and re-analysis of the same experiment remain useful context but do not inflate that count.

## Lane C — authorised site-data pilot opportunities

The first approved site pilot can strengthen transferability and troubleshooting validation, but only if the site actually has suitable de-identified measurements. High-value fields are:

- cavity pressure by cavity;
- screw position, velocity and pressure command versus actual;
- V/P transfer timing and fill-fraction proxy;
- cushion and shot-delivery history;
- TCU supply/return temperature, flow and pressure-drop actuals;
- hot-runner zone actual temperature and heater duty/current;
- valve-gate command and actual actuation evidence;
- tie-bar/mould strain or separation evidence;
- material grade/lot, regrind/recycled fraction, drying and point-of-use moisture where measured;
- shot-linked part mass, dimensions, defect labels and mechanical tests;
- eject-force/load and part/tool temperature;
- timestamped intervention with before/after recovery verification.

No site-data field is required merely to satisfy this queue. Site authority, privacy rules, instrumentation and approved scope control what can be used. Raw proprietary rows remain outside the public repository.

## Promotion and strengthening sequence

For a new or challenged mechanism claim:

1. Deduplicate literature by DOI and experiment identity.
2. Verify peer-reviewed publication and publisher metadata.
3. Record measured signals and physical outcomes.
4. Record machine/tool/material/test context and limitations.
5. Record experiment-independence/overlap information.
6. Require at least two independent publisher-verified primary measured studies before formal mechanism promotion.
7. Require an explicit dossier/overlay transition; discovery alone must never change learner maturity.
8. Link evidence to learning without relabelling synthetic cases as measured data.
9. Where suitable public or authorised data exist, run a separate source-fingerprinted benchmark.
10. Promote only the relationship supported; never turn local numerical results into universal settings.

For an already promoted mechanism, new evidence can strengthen, narrow or challenge scope. It must not silently broaden the promoted claim.
