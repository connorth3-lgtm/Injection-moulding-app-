# MouldMaster learning and engineering review basis

This register documents the public evidence used for the 2026-10-01 deep remediation pass. It is a design/reference register, not a claim that MouldMaster itself has demonstrated the same effect sizes.

## Learning science

- Atkinson RK, Derry SJ, Renkl A, Wortham D. *Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research.* Review of Educational Research. 2000;70(2):181-214. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181  
  **Use in MouldMaster:** novice worked support, explicit solution structure, and integrated prompts.

- Kalyuga S, Ayres P, Chandler P, Sweller J. *The Expertise Reversal Effect.* Educational Psychologist. 2003;38(1):23-31. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_4  
  **Use in MouldMaster:** reduce instructional assistance as prior knowledge increases.

- Roediger HL, Karpicke JD. *Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention.* Psychological Science. 2006;17(3):249-255. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x  
  **Use in MouldMaster:** retrieval practice and delayed review as learning activities, not merely grading.

- Rowland CA. *The effect of testing versus restudy on retention: a meta-analytic review of the testing effect.* Psychological Bulletin. 2014;140(6):1432-1463. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037559  
  **Use in MouldMaster:** retention-oriented review and separation of immediate from delayed performance.

- Mayer RE. *The Multimedia Principle.* In: The Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning, 3rd ed. Cambridge University Press, 2021.  
  **Use in MouldMaster:** prefer relevant explanatory graphics paired with concise text; avoid decorative media and split-attention layouts. Graphic production remains a separate governed asset task and is not treated as complete by this remediation.

- A 2025 meta-analysis of the expertise reversal effect reports that instructional assistance interacts with prior expertise and that assistance for novices is especially important. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102142  
  **Use in MouldMaster:** adaptive guidance bands are pedagogical support, not a change to engineering answer truth.

## Injection-moulding engineering references

The Book continues to use primary standards, manufacturer documentation and peer-reviewed mechanism evidence for publishable claims. The following established books are used as structural completeness references, not as substitutes for claim-level primary evidence:

- Osswald TA, Turng LS, Gramann PJ. *Injection Molding Handbook*. Hanser.
- Kazmer DO. *Injection Mold Design Engineering*. Hanser.
- Beaumont JP. *Runner and Gating Design Handbook*. Hanser.
- Fischer JM. *Handbook of Molded Part Shrinkage and Warpage*. Elsevier/William Andrew.

The remediation specifically checks for coverage of mould/feed-system/cooling/ejection engineering, rheology, differential shrinkage/warpage, measurement-first diagnosis, and worked engineering examples.

## Boundaries

- Published Book claims still follow the governed source hierarchy and exact-byte authorization.
- A learning-science citation justifies an instructional design choice; it does not validate MouldMaster learner outcomes.
- Real learner effectiveness, psychometric validity, independent Book SME approval and practical competence remain external evidence gates.
- No study-specific injection-moulding values are converted into universal production settings.
