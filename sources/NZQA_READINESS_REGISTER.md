# NZQA education-readiness source register

Status date: **2026-09-15**

Purpose: authoritative public-source register for MouldMaster's NZQA education-readiness work. This file supports curriculum and provider-readiness design only. It is **not evidence that NZQA has approved MouldMaster, accredited a provider, granted consent to assess, accepted moderation, awarded credits, or recognised learner/workplace competence**.

## Current NZQA rule baseline

### Micro-credential Approval and Accreditation Rules 2026
- Official source: https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/about-us/rules-fees-policies/nzqa-rules/micro-credential-approval-and-accreditation-rules/
- Commencement: 19 January 2026.
- Use in MouldMaster: title/outcome, need/stakeholder, requirements, review, delivery and assessment/moderation readiness gates.
- Boundary: repository evidence can support an application; approval/accreditation are external NZQA/provider outcomes.

### Qualification and Micro-credential Listing and Operational Rules 2026
- Official source: https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/about-us/rules-fees-policies/nzqa-rules/qualification-and-micro-credentials/
- Use in MouldMaster: listing/operational context, credit and review governance.
- Current micro-credential size: 1–40 credits.
- Credit basis: one credit represents 10 notional learning hours.
- Boundary: do not derive credits from screen time or the existing number of lessons.

### Consent to Assess Against Standards on the Directory of Assessment and Skill Standards Rules 2026
- Official source: https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/ncea/ncea-rules-and-procedures/consent-to-assess/
- Use in MouldMaster: fail-closed gate whenever DASS standards are proposed for formal assessment.
- Boundary: curriculum mapping is not consent to assess.

### Consent and Moderation Requirements (CMRs)
- Official source: https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/assessment-and-moderation-of-standards/consent-and-moderation-requirements/
- Use in MouldMaster: provider capability, assessor/resource/practical-experience and national external moderation boundary.
- Injection Moulding standards listed below reference **CMR 13** on the NZQA domain page.
- Boundary: MouldMaster cannot self-grant moderation acceptance or provider capability.

### Assessment of standards
- Official source: https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/tertiary/assessment-and-moderation-of-standards/assessment/
- Use in MouldMaster: distinguish learning opportunities and formative evidence from provider-controlled assessment decisions.
- Boundary: app completion, quiz scores and simulations do not become NZQA results by themselves.

### Mapping rule
A Book chapter may provide supporting knowledge or practice context relevant to a standard. That does **not** mean the chapter covers every outcome, evidence requirement, range statement, explanatory note, practical condition, CMR requirement, assessor requirement or moderation obligation of that standard.

The controlled chapter/outcome/standard map is stored in:
- `data/nzqa-education-readiness-v1.json`

## Evidence-state boundary

Repository-controlled evidence can support:
- draft learning outcomes and chapter mappings;
- versioned technical content and source trails;
- formative knowledge checks and case practice;
- draft summative instruments and marking structures for provider review;
- draft practical-observation templates;
- privacy-conscious evidence field definitions;
- curriculum, source and QA traceability.

External/provider evidence remains required for:
- eligible provider/application ownership;
- final NZQCF level and credits;
- documented need and stakeholder/industry support;
- approved admission, RPL/credit and completion rules;
- authorised assessors/verifiers/moderators;
- consent to assess where DASS standards are used;
- internal moderation and national external moderation participation;
- real workplace practical evidence;
- learner identity, enrolment, support, complaints/appeals and recognised record reporting;
- final NZQA approval/accreditation.

## Recheck rule

Immediately before any real provider submission or standards-based assessment design, recheck:
1. the live 2026 NZQA rules and any amendments/replacements;
2. the Injection Moulding domain status and each selected standard/version;
3. the applicable CMR and standard-setting body requirements;
4. qualification status if used for contextual benchmarking;
5. provider eligibility, consent-to-assess scope and moderation obligations.
