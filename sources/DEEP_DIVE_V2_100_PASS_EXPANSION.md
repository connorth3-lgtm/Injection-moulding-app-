## Promotion boundary

- The 100 rows above count **research passes**, not 100 approved papers.
- A DOI or repository record is not automatically E2/E4 evidence. Full-text relevance, measured-vs-simulated status, experimental context and permitted claims still have to be checked.
- Search results that were patents, generic manufacturing papers, simulation-only work or weakly matched records were not promoted merely to increase counts.
- Repeated discovery of an already-seeded source strengthens prioritisation but does not increase the unique-paper count.
- Dataset candidates remain candidates until licence, availability, schema, units, cycle identity, sensor location, missingness, synchronisation and quality labels are independently profiled.
- Embargoed data must remain outside the verified-usable count until the embargo expires and access/rights are rechecked.

## Immediate modelling opportunities surfaced

1. **Injection-unit health tree:** NRV wear/backflow, screw wear, plasticising torque/power and recovery instability can now be separated using pressure, torque, displacement, flow-rate and recovery-time evidence instead of treating cushion variation as a single cause.
2. **Clamp/tool mechanics tree:** tie-bar imbalance, mould separation, core deflection and ejection force can become discriminating branches with physical measurements.
3. **Cooling-health tree:** coolant hydraulics, thermal contact resistance, conformal-cooling performance and venting can be represented as measured tooling mechanisms rather than generic temperature advice.
4. **Material-condition tree:** moisture, batch rheology, recycling loops and fibre attrition provide material-state mechanisms that can be distinguished from machine faults.
5. **Cross-machine diagnostics:** transfer-learning and machine-fingerprint studies support a future layer that explicitly separates invariant process physics from machine-specific signal baselines.
6. **Visual + waveform fusion:** the new defect-image candidate, when its embargo permits access, could be linked to process/energy records and existing multimodal waveform datasets for evidence-backed multimodal diagnostic cases.
