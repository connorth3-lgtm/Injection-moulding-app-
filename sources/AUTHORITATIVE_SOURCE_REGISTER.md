# MouldMaster Academy — Authoritative Source Register

Status date: 2026-08-24

Purpose: maintain a transparent source set for lesson enrichment, the searchable reference database, safety/legal reference pages and content review. These sources **support mechanisms and obligations; they do not create universal process recipes**. Grade-specific supplier data, machine manuals, mould documentation, approved site procedures and applicable law control specific production limits.

Assessment rule: this source register must **not** be used to inject answer-supporting references into an assessment before grading. Exact question sources may be shown in post-grade debriefs only.

## Reference-database coverage

The in-app **Reference Data** database is deliberately organized as a mechanism-and-evidence guide rather than a universal recipe table. Its categories map to this register as follows:

- **Materials** → process/material test methods, polymer rheology, crystallisation and manufacturer grade guidance.
- **Defects** → process/material references plus filling, venting, packing, cooling, shrinkage and tooling evidence.
- **Process signals** → process-monitoring, cavity-pressure, sensor and machine-response references.
- **Tooling** → Moldflow tooling/filling/cooling references, tooling condition monitoring and applicable machine/tool safety controls.
- **Machine** → ISO 20430, HSE/OSHA machinery guidance, machine-specific manuals and process-monitoring references.
- **Quality** → NIST engineering statistics and ISO 22514 capability/measurement references.
- **Safety** → ISO 20430 plus current regulator and legal sources for the applicable jurisdiction.
- **Troubleshooting** → evidence from the relevant materials, tooling, process, sensor and statistics categories; no troubleshooting card should be read as a universal setting instruction.
- **Glossary** → terminology distilled from the same technical source families and validated machine/material documentation.

## Source-status rules

1. **Law/regulator pages control legal statements**, not secondary articles.
2. **ISO/ASTM pages identify standards**, but full standard text may require purchase/licensed access.
3. **Peer-reviewed sources support mechanisms and research findings**, not universal machine settings.
4. **Manufacturer guides are material/product-context guidance** and must not be generalised to every grade or machine.
5. **Simulation/vendor resources are explanatory technical sources**, not proof that a specific production process is validated.
6. **Legacy WorkSafe plastics guidance is supplementary only** and must be visibly labelled as not updated for current HSWA legislation.
7. **Machine, resin and hot-runner supplier documentation controls equipment/grade-specific limits** where it is more specific than this general learning database.
8. Recheck time-sensitive legal, standard and manufacturer-source status before every formal accreditation submission or major release.
