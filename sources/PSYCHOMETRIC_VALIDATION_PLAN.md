# Psychometric validation plan

This plan prevents MouldMaster's current short learning checks from being overstated as a validated professional-competence examination.

## Current boundary

The normal regional form samples seven technical items plus regional safety/compliance items. The current >=80% overall rule plus zero-wrong safety-critical rule remains a **learning-completion policy**. It is not an empirically established professional competence cut score.

The prepared 8–15 learner pilot may evaluate usability, immediate learning signals, delayed transfer/retention and diagnostic reasoning. It cannot validate stable item discrimination, test reliability, a competence cut score or credential validity.

## Pre-registered calibration gates

For operational item calibration, retain stable item ID + revision and use real independent first-attempt responses. The planning floor is 100 responses per item, with uncertainty reported for item difficulty and discrimination. This is a conservative planning minimum rather than a universal theorem; more data may be required for extreme items, subgroup analysis or high-stakes use.

For a separately governed summative form, the planning floor is 200 complete independent first-attempt forms before test-level reliability/precision is considered for operational interpretation. A valid programme also needs adequate domain sampling, construct/dimensionality review, uncertainty around scores and attention to misclassification risk.

## Cut-score boundary

Before any competence-bearing cut score:
- define the competence claim and target population;
- use a documented standard-setting method;
- involve qualified independent injection-moulding and assessment reviewers;
- report uncertainty/sensitivity around the proposed standard;
- demonstrate adequate content-domain sampling;
- keep safety-critical mastery separate from the overall-score calculation.

The current 80% learning-completion policy is not evidence that 80% is the correct professional competence threshold.

## Independence

Synthetic traffic, automated browsers, developer accounts and AI review never count as psychometric validation. Real-response evidence and independent review remain required.
