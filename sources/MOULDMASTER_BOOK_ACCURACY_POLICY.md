# MouldMaster Book accuracy policy

Status: foundation policy for the Book workstream.

The MouldMaster Book is a governed technical learning surface. Accuracy has priority over chapter count, speed of publication, or narrative completeness.

## Evidence states

Every chapter is one of: `planned`, `source-review`, `technical-review`, `verified`, or `hold`.

`verified` is a release claim. It must not be inferred from the fact that text already exists elsewhere in MouldMaster. Existing canonical lessons are candidate educational source material only until their Book claims have been checked against the evidence rules below.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. applicable standards and official standards-body records;
2. current material-manufacturer processing guides, grade-specific TDS and SDS information;
3. current machine, controller, hot-runner and mould-component manufacturer documentation;
4. peer-reviewed polymer/process research;
5. established injection-moulding engineering references;
6. governed MouldMaster lesson content after technical review.

A lower-ranked source must not override a more directly applicable current primary source without an explicit rationale.

## Applicability

Every substantive claim must be classified as one or more of:

- fundamental;
- standard-defined;
- manufacturer-specific;
- grade-specific;
- machine-specific;
- mould-specific;
- measured/experimental;
- diagnostic hypothesis.

Material-specific processing values must not be generalized across a polymer family when grade-specific evidence is required. Machine limits, mould limits, hot-runner limits, supplier limits, approved site procedures and current safety instructions take precedence over generic Book guidance.

## Numbers and settings

A technical number is not allowed into a verified chapter unless it has:

- a unit;
- an applicability statement;
- a traceable source or declared measured/experimental basis;
- enough context to prevent a typical or example value from being read as a universal setpoint.

Grade-specific settings require grade-specific evidence. Unsupported numbers fail closed: remove them, keep the chapter in review, or state the uncertainty.

## Troubleshooting

The Book teaches troubleshooting as:

**symptom -> plausible mechanism -> evidence to check -> controlled test -> interpretation -> possible corrective action**.

It must not teach symptom-to-fix tables as guaranteed causal rules. A corrective action remains a hypothesis until the relevant mechanism is supported by process, material, mould or machine evidence.

## Safety

Safety chapters require authoritative safety sources. The Book does not replace current machine manuals, mould/hot-runner instructions, SDS requirements, lockout/tagout or isolation procedures, workplace procedures, or applicable law/standards.

No Book text may encourage bypassing guards, interlocks, rated machine limits, temperature/pressure limits, or site controls.

## Conflicting or incomplete evidence

Where credible sources disagree:

1. check scope, revision, material grade, equipment, test method and conditions;
2. prefer the source that is more directly applicable to the claim;
3. document the reason for the chosen interpretation;
4. if the conflict remains material, mark the claim/chapter `hold` rather than inventing certainty.

## Source currency

A source supporting `verified` status must be checked for current/superseded state. Manufacturer documents must retain issuer, document/grade identity where available, revision/date where available, URL/provenance, and the date MouldMaster checked the source.

## Audio integrity

The spoken Book and readable Book must use the same governed chapter content. The audio layer must not add technical advice, numbers, or simplified causal claims that are absent from the governed text.

## Initial anchor sources

The first manifest records current official/public anchors including ISO 20430:2020, ISO 12165:2019, ASTM D3641-24, and a current BASF Ultramid processing guide. These anchors establish safety, terminology and source-first processing principles; they do not make every planned chapter verified.

## Release rule

If the evidence cannot support the wording safely, the Book says less.
