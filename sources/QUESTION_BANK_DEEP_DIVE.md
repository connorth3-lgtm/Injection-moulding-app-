# MouldMaster question-and-answer deep dive

Reviewed: 24 August 2026

## Scope

This review examined all 57 live exam questions (30 technical and 27 UK/US/NZ regional safety/compliance items) and all 16 troubleshooting scenario drills. The review went beyond structural answer-key checks and challenged wording, competing mechanisms, distractor plausibility, rationale quality, source fit, difficulty progression and the risk of teaching an unsafe shortcut through a distractor.

The review deliberately did **not** change regional safety/compliance answer keys. Current official-source checks still support the existing jurisdiction logic. Twelve technical questions and eight scenario drills were rewritten to improve diagnostic reasoning while preserving their intended learning objective and correct-answer concept.

## Main findings and corrections

1. **Definition spotting was too easy in several items.** Higher-level questions now use realistic evidence conflicts, for example stable machine peak pressure versus a changing local cavity-pressure trace.
2. **Some distractors were implausible or too obviously wrong.** Revised distractors are now credible competing diagnoses while remaining clearly non-recommended once feedback is shown.
3. **Gate-seal wording needed more qualification.** A part-mass plateau is presented as strong evidence of effective gate seal under a stable tested condition and adequate measurement resolution, not as an absolute universal gate-freeze time.
4. **MFR needed stronger treatment.** The bank now explicitly tests that MFR is a standardized single-condition flow measure and not a complete description of injection-moulding rheology or mouldability.
5. **Capability needed process-structure awareness.** The advanced bank now tests that pooled cavity data can hide cavity-specific mean/variation structure and that stability/measurement/rational-subgroup reasoning comes before relying on one capability number.
6. **DOE reasoning needed a real confounding case.** Randomisation/blocking is now tested through factor level being aligned with early/late shift time rather than through definition recall.
7. **Machine transfer needed physical-process reasoning.** The advanced transfer item now distinguishes matching process outputs/material conditions from copying machine-specific numerical settings.
8. **Scenario feedback is now mechanism-specific.** The eight added scenario drills no longer use the same generic wrong-answer message for every distractor.

- WorkSafe New Zealand machine-lockout guidance continues to require safe/effective isolation before servicing where reasonably practicable and harmful unexpected movement/energy is possible: https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/machinery/keeping-workers-safe-with-machine-lockouts/

## Question-design rules retained

- One defensible best answer per exam item.
- Four distinct options per exam item.
- Regional questions remain safety-critical.
- Wrong answers are distractors, not authorised procedures.
- No universal resin drying, barrel-temperature, pressure, speed, clamp or cooling setting is taught as a rule.
- Technical explanations state the physical/statistical mechanism and explain why the nearest competing answer is weaker.
- Research results are evidence of mechanisms or methods, not automatic local production settings.
- Supplier grade data, machine/mould documentation, approved procedures, applicable law and product-specific validation remain controlling.
