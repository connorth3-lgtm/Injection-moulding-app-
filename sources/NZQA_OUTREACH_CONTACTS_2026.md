# NZQA outreach contact routes — checked 2026-09-15

This source note records **public contact routes only**. It does not record contact, agreement, endorsement, approval, accreditation, consent to assess, moderation acceptance or provider commitment.

## Manufacturing and Engineering Industry Skills Board (MAEISB)

- Contact: https://maeisb.nz/contact-us/
- Qualifications / standards / training schemes (micro-credentials): `qualifications@maeisb.nz`
- National external moderation / consent to assess / quality assurance: `moderation@maeisb.nz`
- General enquiries: `info@maeisb.nz`
- Phone: 04 280 3378
- Industry coverage includes plastics, polymer and rubber product manufacturing.

- Get in touch: https://www.competenz.org.nz/contact-us/get-in-touch
- Offices/contact: https://www.competenz.org.nz/contact-us/our-offices
- Phone: 0800 526 1800 / +64 9 539 9859
- Head office listed publicly: EMA Building, 145 Khyber Pass Road, Grafton, Auckland 1023
- Public plastics page directs prospective users to **Talk to an advisor**.
- TEC status: https://www.tec.govt.nz/strategic-initiatives/vocational-education-system/delivering-vocational-education/work-based-learning-divisions

Use the public business contact route for MouldMaster pathway questions rather than a learner-support inbox or an inferred/private staff address.

## Plastics New Zealand

- Contact: https://www.plastics.org.nz/about-us/contact-us
- General public email: `info@plastics.org.nz`

Use this route for industry-needs evidence, employer-review introductions and consultation advice. Contact does not establish endorsement.

## Evidence boundary

A public contact route establishes only where an enquiry can be sent. Sending a message records a contact attempt. A reply must be classified using `certification/NZQA_EXTERNAL_RESPONSE_TRIAGE_2026.md` before any governed readiness status is changed.
