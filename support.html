App does not update

For the installed PWA, connect to the internet, open MouldMaster, close it fully and reopen it. Offline content is refreshed through the service worker after a successful online check.

Windows update blocked safely

If the Windows updater says an update was blocked, keep the current installed copy. The updater is designed to reject content that fails its validation checks rather than replace a known-good copy.

Progress and backups

Use the app's export function to create a progress backup before changing browser profiles, clearing site data or moving devices. Imported backups are validated and certificates are intentionally re-earned on the receiving device.

Certificates

Current certificates are MouldMaster learning-completion records. They are not yet NZQA-approved qualifications, IACET CEUs, statutory authorisation or proof of machine-specific workplace competence.

Safety or technical concern

Do not use the app to override a machine safeguard, site isolation procedure, resin supplier instruction or applicable legal requirement. If you identify a potentially incorrect safety/technical item, stop relying on that item until it has been reviewed against the cited/current source.

Publisher support contact

A public support email/address must be inserted by the publisher before Microsoft Store submission. The repository currently has no verified public support mailbox configured.
