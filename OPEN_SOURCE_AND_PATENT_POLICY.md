# MouldMaster Academy — Open Source & Patent Policy

## Project intent

MouldMaster Academy is intended to remain openly available, auditable and modifiable.

The project uses the **Apache License 2.0** for repository-owned source code, documentation and original assets unless a file says otherwise. Apache-2.0 includes an express patent licence from contributors for patent claims necessarily infringed by their contributions.

## No project patenting policy

For contributions owned and controlled by the MouldMaster project maintainers:

- the maintainers do not intend to seek patent protection covering the implementation contributed to this public repository;
- the maintainers intend to make project-owned implementation available for open use under Apache-2.0;
- contributors must not knowingly add material they do not have the right to license;
- contributors should disclose known patent restrictions, standards-essential patent concerns or third-party licensing obligations that could affect use of a contribution.

This policy is a project commitment. It is **not a warranty that no third-party patent exists anywhere in the world**. Patent clearance requires jurisdiction-specific professional searching and legal analysis.

## Third-party patents and standards

A public standard, technical method, research paper or manufacturer guide may describe technology that is patented, formerly patented or subject to other rights. Linking to such a source does not grant patent rights.

The project therefore:

1. does not claim worldwide freedom-to-operate;
2. avoids deliberately copying proprietary implementations where an open approach is available;
3. prefers open standards and openly licensed libraries where practical;
4. records third-party dependencies and rights in `NOTICE` / `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`;
5. treats ISO, ASTM and similar standards as references only unless the relevant text is separately licensed for redistribution.

## Open-source completeness

A distributed component should only be advertised as fully open source when the corresponding preferred source form and reasonable build instructions are available under an open-source licence.

At the time this policy was added, the web/PWA source is present in the repository. The legacy `MouldMasterAcademy.exe` launcher binary is present, but its corresponding launcher source/build recipe has not been located in the repository. Therefore that binary must not yet be marketed as a fully open-source component.

## Contributions

Unless explicitly marked otherwise before submission, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion in this project are accepted under Apache License 2.0, including its contributor patent grant.

If a contributor cannot make that grant, the contribution should not be merged without explicit legal/licensing review.
