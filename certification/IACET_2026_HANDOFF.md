# IACET 2026 Accreditation Handoff

Status: repository preparation / external-action handoff. **MouldMaster Academy is not an IACET Accredited Provider and does not issue IACET CEUs.**

Verified against IACET public information: **2026-08-24**.

## Exact next decision

Before spending money on IACET accreditation, identify the **legal organisation that would apply** and verify that it satisfies IACET's public eligibility gate:

- legally recognised organisation/business entity (or other eligible applicant form)
- at least one year in business
- operating under the application conditions for at least three months
- a defined organisational unit with authority/responsibility for the continuing-education and training system

If those conditions cannot yet be evidenced, keep building the operating evidence binder and pilot history rather than purchasing the application early.

## Current 2026 cost/process checkpoint

Public IACET information checked on 2026-08-24 lists:

- ANSI/IACET 1-2018 Standard + Initial Application bundle: **USD 495**
- Initial Application Review Fee: **USD 4,845**
- Accredited Provider Annual Maintenance Fee: **USD 1,245**
- purchased application validity: **one year**
- typical review: approximately **3–4 months**

Recheck fees and terms immediately before purchase.

## Low-cost / no-cost actions before application purchase

1. Use IACET's free Accreditation Readiness Calculator.
2. Review IACET's public Application Process, Eligibility, FAQ and Application Resources pages.
3. Attend a free Introduction to IACET Accreditation webinar and/or Accreditation Assistance Forum if useful.
4. Confirm the applicant legal entity and one-year operating-history evidence.
5. Define the organisational CE/T unit and name the accountable accreditation owner.
6. Run a real adult-learning pilot/operating cycle under the proposed policies.
7. Populate `IACET_READINESS_MATRIX.md` with links/locations for each real evidence item.
8. Only then consider purchasing the Standard + Application bundle for a clause-level self-audit.

## Evidence that must become organisational, not just repository documentation

The application organisation should be able to show that its CE/T management system actually operates. Priority evidence includes:

- approved quality/CE-T policies with owners and revision control
- real needs-analysis records and stakeholder evidence
- real learning-event design file(s)
- named instructional personnel and competence evidence
- learner support/accessibility records
- real enrolment/participation/completion records in a controlled provider system
- completed assessments and decision records
- learner/event evaluation responses and analysis
- corrective actions and effectiveness checks
- management review showing decisions made from the evidence

Draft templates without operating evidence should be treated as preparation only.

## CEU/credential boundary

One CEU is publicly defined as ten contact hours, but CEU arithmetic alone does not create an IACET CEU. MouldMaster must not calculate or market **IACET CEUs** until Accredited Provider status has been granted and the learning event is administered through the accredited organisation's compliant system.

The CEU is a unit of continuing-education participation, not a licence, professional certification or qualification by itself.
