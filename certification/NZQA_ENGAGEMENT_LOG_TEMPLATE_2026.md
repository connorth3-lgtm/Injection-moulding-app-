## Fail-closed rule

Only advance a status when documentary evidence supports that exact status. Never convert:
- public marketing into partnership;
- meeting attendance into endorsement;
- provider interest into accreditation;
- curriculum mapping into consent to assess;
- formative app evidence into recognised assessment;
- simulation into workplace competence;
- CI success into NZQA approval or publication authority.
