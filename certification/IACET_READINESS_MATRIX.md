# IACET Accreditation Readiness Matrix — 2026 Baseline

Status: internal readiness document. **MouldMaster Academy is not currently an IACET Accredited Provider and does not issue IACET CEUs.**

Verified baseline date: **2026-08-24**.

This document maps repository evidence to IACET's publicly described accreditation framework. It is not a substitute for the purchased ANSI/IACET Standard or the official application. The current public IACET accreditation pages continue to identify **ANSI/IACET 1-2018** as the operative CE/T Standard. A standards-development proposal or draft must not be treated as an effective accreditation requirement until IACET formally adopts it.

## 1. Eligibility gate — must be satisfied before paying for an application

IACET accreditation is organisational accreditation for providers of adult continuing education and training; IACET does not approve a single course independently of Accredited Provider status.

The intended applicant must be able to demonstrate all of the following before MouldMaster represents itself as application-ready:

- a governmental unit, incorporated/chartered organisation, or other legally recognised business entity (including an eligible legally recognised sole proprietorship)
- at least **one year in business**
- operation under the conditions described in the application for at least **three months**
- a defined organisational unit responsible for CE/T, with authority, responsibility and administrative control
- written policies/processes and supporting evidence suitable for application review and the accreditation interview

**Current MouldMaster status:** legal-entity eligibility, operating history and the applicant organisational unit have not been verified in this repository. This is an external owner/legal-entity gate.

## 2. Current public IACET process/cost baseline

Verified from IACET public pages on 2026-08-24:

- Standard + Initial Application bundle: **USD 495**
- initial application review fee: **USD 4,845** (non-refundable)
- Accredited Provider annual maintenance fee: **USD 1,245**
- the purchased Standard/application bundle is valid for **one year** from purchase
- typical accreditation review is approximately **3–4 months**, depending on additional-information requests and interview scheduling
- accreditation interviews are conducted virtually by default
- Accredited Providers submit ongoing maintenance/annual reporting and reaccredit on a **five-year** cycle

Fees and process details can change. Recheck IACET directly immediately before purchase or payment.

## 4. CEU boundary

The public CEU definition remains **1 CEU = 10 contact hours** of qualifying organised continuing education/training participation.

This does **not** mean MouldMaster may divide app time by ten and advertise IACET CEUs. Before any IACET CEU is awarded, the accredited organisation must determine and document qualifying contact hours under its approved system, account for non-qualifying time, confirm completion/assessment requirements and maintain the required learner record.

Until IACET grants Accredited Provider status, MouldMaster must not:

- label a certificate or badge as an **IACET CEU**
- claim that a course is **IACET approved**
- claim that the organisation is **IACET accredited**
- use the IACET Accredited Provider designation/logo without permission
- imply that existing local completion certificates are IACET credentials

A CEU is a unit of continuing-education participation; it is not itself a professional credential or licence.

## 5. Evidence binder to assemble before application

1. Legal entity documentation and operating-history evidence.
2. Defined applicant organisational unit, organisation chart and named accreditation owner.
3. Approved CE/T quality manual and controlled policy register.
4. Training-needs-analysis report with real stakeholder evidence.
5. One complete learning-event design file showing need → outcomes → content/methods → assessment → evaluation.
6. Instructional personnel/SME/instructor/assessor competence records.
7. Learning-environment, accessibility, support and contingency procedures.
8. Assessment blueprint, instruments, marking/decision rules, reassessment and appeal controls.
9. Learner identity/participation and completion controls appropriate to the delivery model.
10. Provider-controlled learner-record, privacy, security, access/correction/release and retention procedures.
11. CEU calculation procedure to be finalised only against the purchased Standard/application and approved organisational system.
12. Learner/event evaluation instruments and completed real examples.
13. Corrective-action / continuous-improvement log with effectiveness checks.
14. Marketing/credential-use policy blocking premature IACET claims.
15. Completed pilot/operating evidence showing the written system is actually used, not merely drafted.

## 6. Recommended pre-purchase sequence

1. Verify the intended applicant entity meets the legal-status and operating-history gates.
2. Define the organisational unit that would hold Accredited Provider status.
3. Use IACET's free Accreditation Readiness Calculator and public application resources.
4. Run the MouldMaster evidence binder against the nine public categories.
5. Conduct at least one real adult-learning pilot/operating cycle using the proposed policies and retain evidence.
6. Resolve major evidence gaps before purchasing the Standard/application bundle.
7. When organisational readiness is credible, purchase the current Standard + Initial Application bundle and perform a clause-level self-audit against the purchased material.
8. Submit only when the organisation can support the review fee, annual maintenance obligations, interview and ongoing compliance/annual reporting.

## 7. Accreditation maintenance planning

If accreditation is later granted, the organisation must treat it as an ongoing management system rather than a one-off certificate. Planning should include:

- continuing conformity with the applicable IACET Standard
- annual reporting and annual maintenance fees
- controlled changes to delivery methods/content
- learner-record stewardship
- periodic internal review/audit and corrective action
- five-year reaccreditation planning
- immediate removal/correction of IACET claims if authority to issue IACET CEUs lapses

## 8. Project gate

Repository readiness can prepare policies, evidence structures, QA and claim controls. It cannot establish the applicant's legal eligibility, prove one-year operating history, create historical pilot evidence, purchase the Standard, submit the application, participate in the accreditation interview or obtain IACET's decision.

**Do not configure MouldMaster to calculate, display or issue IACET CEUs until IACET has granted Accredited Provider status and the accredited organisation has confirmed the learning event and record process comply with its approved system.**
