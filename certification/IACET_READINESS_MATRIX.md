# IACET Accreditation Readiness Matrix

Status: internal readiness document. **MouldMaster Academy is not currently an IACET Accredited Provider and does not issue IACET CEUs.**

Target: organisational accreditation against the current ANSI/IACET 1-2018 continuing-education and training standard and IACET application process.

| Readiness area | Current evidence | Gap / next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Organisational eligibility | Project has a functioning training product and version-controlled repository | Confirm applicant legal entity/recognised sole proprietorship, operating history and ownership/governance documentation |
| Responsibility & governance | Quality principles and release controls documented in `QUALITY_AND_ASSESSMENT_MANUAL.md` | Approve named accountable roles, document authority/delegation and annual management review |
| Learning-needs analysis | Curriculum reflects operator-to-expert injection-moulding development | Conduct documented employer/learner needs analysis with dated evidence and target-audience definitions |
| Learning outcomes | Draft level outcomes and NZQA concept outcomes documented | Map every learning event/assessment to measurable outcomes and control outcome revisions |
| Instructional design | 120-lesson curriculum, cases, quizzes, references, spaced review | Create formal design template showing needs → outcomes → content → activity → assessment alignment |
| Qualified instructional personnel | SME review recommended | Appoint named SMEs/instructional designer and retain competence/CV evidence and review records |
| Learning environment/resources | PWA/Windows training platform, offline capability | Document technical support, accessibility, contingency, learner prerequisites and platform QA |
| Assessment | Audited bank, rationales, 80% + zero critical-safety rule, applied scenarios | Define summative blueprint, assessor marking rules, reassessment, identity controls and evidence retention |
| Evaluation | Confidence and weak-area signals exist | Implement structured learner reaction, learning, application/employer feedback and annual trend review |
| Records | Local learner records/backups/versioning exist | Move recognised records to provider-controlled secure system with retention, privacy, access and correction policies |
| CEU calculation | Not implemented | Define eligible instructional hours using IACET rules only after accreditation advice; do not award CEUs beforehand |
| Complaints/appeals | Draft assessment appeal concept in quality manual | Approve formal complaints, appeals, response time, escalation and recordkeeping policy |
| Continuous improvement | QA workflow and standards-change checks exist | Add documented management review, corrective actions, effectiveness checks and evidence repository |
| Marketing integrity | Current app states certificates are local/non-accredited | Formal marketing approval gate preventing premature use of “IACET”, “CEU”, “accredited”, “certified” claims |

## Evidence binder to assemble
1. Legal entity and operating-history evidence.
2. Organisation chart and named accreditation owner.
3. Quality/assessment manual approved and signed.
4. Training-needs-analysis report and stakeholder evidence.
5. One complete programme-design file showing alignment from need to evaluation.
6. SME/instructor/assessor competence records.
7. Assessment blueprint, instruments, marking schedules and moderation samples.
8. Learner-support and accessibility procedure.
9. Record/privacy/retention procedure.
10. Complaints and appeals procedure.
11. Evaluation instruments and completed pilot examples.
12. Continuous-improvement/corrective-action log.
13. Marketing and credential-use policy.
14. Sample attendance/completion record and certificate clearly marked non-IACET during pre-accreditation.

## Pilot recommendation
Before application, run a controlled pilot with real injection-moulding learners and employers. Retain:
- enrolment/eligibility evidence
- completion data
- item-level assessment results
- learner feedback
- supervisor/application feedback
- moderation sample
- corrective actions and revised material

## Accreditation gate
Do not configure the app to calculate, display or issue IACET CEUs until IACET has granted Accredited Provider status and the organisation has confirmed the programme/record process conforms to its approved system.
