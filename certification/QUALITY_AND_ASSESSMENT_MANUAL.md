# MouldMaster Academy — Quality & Assessment Manual

Document status: controlled draft for external review. This manual does **not** itself confer accreditation.

## 1. Purpose
MouldMaster Academy provides structured injection-moulding theory, troubleshooting, safety-awareness and process-engineering learning from beginner to advanced level. It is designed to support workplace learning, not replace machine-specific instruction, employer authorisation, statutory duties, supplier documentation or practical supervision.

## 2. Governance principles
1. Safety-critical content takes precedence over engagement/gamification.
2. Legal and standards content is jurisdiction-specific and date/version controlled.
3. Technical claims should be source-backed or explicitly identified as engineering guidance.
4. Material-specific production limits come from the current resin-grade supplier TDS/SDS and approved site controls, not universal recipes.
5. Assessment pass logic cannot be overridden by XP, ranks, streaks, achievements or other gamification.
6. Changes to assessment keys or safety-critical content require documented review before release.

## 3. Current assessment architecture
Current audited question-bank version: `2026.08.21.1`.

Knowledge assessment rules:
- UK/US/NZ mode: 10 questions (7 technical + 3 regional safety questions).
- Compare All: 16 questions (7 technical + all 9 regional safety questions).
- Pass requirement: at least 80% overall **and zero incorrect safety-critical regional questions**.
- Correct answers are never changed by confidence scoring, spaced repetition, XP or practical sign-off.

## 4. Learning outcomes framework
Each level should evidence progression across these domains:

### Foundation
- describe the injection-moulding cycle and major machine/mould/material functions
- distinguish controller setpoints from measured process actuals
- identify common hazards and the need for effective safeguards/isolation
- recognise basic defect mechanisms and appropriate evidence to collect

### Applied / Intermediate
- interpret process evidence across Material–Machine–Mould–Method–Measurement
- conduct or explain controlled studies such as partial fills, cavity balance and gate-seal checks
- troubleshoot common defects using competing hypotheses rather than recipe changes
- apply grade-specific material handling and current supplier documentation

### Advanced
- interpret capability/measurement evidence and experimental designs
- transfer processes by reproducing relevant outputs rather than copying controller numbers
- integrate sensors, automation and validation evidence
- justify a robust process window and confirmation strategy

## 5. Assessment design rules
Every summative question should:
- have one defensible best answer
- avoid answer-length and absolute-language cues
- avoid unsafe distractors that appear as recommended actions
- explain why the best answer is best
- explain why weaker options are weaker where practical
- cite an exact source for legal/safety claims where applicable
- avoid universal numeric process recipes unless the number is a defined standard/test condition

## 6. Safety-critical questions
Regional safety items are treated as critical because a high overall score must not compensate for misunderstanding a safeguarding or hazardous-energy duty.

Critical-item rules:
- all applicable regional critical items must be correct to pass
- failed safeguards are never reframed as production troubleshooting variables
- emergency stop is not presented as hazardous-energy isolation
- servicing/maintenance content directs the learner to authorised site procedures and applicable law

## 7. Moderation
### Internal moderation
Before release:
- schema/answer-key check
- technical correctness review
- regional legal/source review
- safety-pedagogy review
- distractor/cue analysis
- reference-link/status review
- regression test of pass/fail logic

### External moderation target
At least annually, or after a material standards/legal revision:
- one independent injection-moulding process SME
- one safety/engineering reviewer familiar with the target jurisdiction(s)
- a polymer/material specialist for material-science modules
- assessor/training-design reviewer for learning outcomes and assessment validity

External reviewers should declare conflicts of interest and sign/date the review record.

## 8. Practical competency
Online knowledge completion does not by itself demonstrate workplace competence. Practical sign-off should require direct observation by a competent workplace supervisor/assessor against defined tasks, including safeguarding behaviour, machine-specific knowledge, evidence collection and escalation decisions.

Practical records must never imply legal authorisation to operate equipment unless the employer's own authorisation process separately grants it.

## 9. Identity and assessment integrity — accreditation target
For externally recognised credentials, implement:
- verified learner identity
- controlled enrolment record
- attempt timestamps and assessment version
- randomised question delivery
- defined reassessment rules
- assessor identity for practical evidence
- tamper-evident credential records
- appeals/review process
- retained assessment evidence and moderation records

The present local-only app is not sufficient by itself for high-assurance identity or credential verification.

## 10. Records
Target retained fields for externally recognised learning records:
- learner legal/preferred name
- unique learner ID
- programme/micro-credential title and version
- enrolment/completion dates
- assessment version and result
- critical-safety result
- practical-signoff status and assessor
- moderation/version history
- credential status (active/revoked/superseded)

Retention periods must be set by the approved provider's legal/regulatory obligations before launch.

## 11. Appeals and reassessment — draft policy
Learners should be able to:
1. request clarification of a result
2. challenge a question where the source or wording is believed incorrect
3. have the item independently reviewed
4. receive the review outcome and correction if required
5. reattempt under the programme's controlled reassessment policy

Safety-critical items should not be waived to create a pass.

## 12. Change control
Every production content release should record:
- release ID/date
- affected lessons/questions/sources
- reason for change
- reviewer(s)
- whether the change affects learning outcomes, assessment validity or legal/safety meaning
- regression-test result

Assessment-key changes trigger a new question-bank version.

## 13. Continuous improvement
Collect and review:
- item difficulty and discrimination where sample size allows
- common wrong-answer patterns
- completion/dropout points
- learner confidence vs correctness
- workplace supervisor feedback
- source/legal changes
- accessibility issues
- incident/near-miss feedback relevant to training design

Improvement decisions must be documented and cannot weaken the safety-critical pass gate for convenience.
