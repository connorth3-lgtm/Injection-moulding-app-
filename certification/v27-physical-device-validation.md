- Learner A/B isolation, learner rename stability, duplicate display names, and ambiguous legacy-bucket quarantine.
- Learner cannot self-promote to instructor, cannot create trainer sign-off, and cannot read another learner’s practical evidence.

- Strict CSV: quoted commas and escaped quotes accepted; unterminated quotes, duplicate normalized headers, ragged rows and quotes in unquoted fields rejected atomically.
- Search: typo/synonym checks including `temprature`, short shot/no fill, mould/mold, NRV/check ring and gate seal/hold-time plateau.
- Multimodal Flash sequence uses trainer/process-lead escalation rather than instructing a global clamp-force change.
