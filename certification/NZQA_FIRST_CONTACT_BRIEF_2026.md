# NZQA first-contact brief — 2026

Status: **draft external-engagement brief only**. No contact, endorsement, approval, accreditation, consent to assess, moderation acceptance or provider commitment is implied.

## Primary ask to MAEISB

MouldMaster Academy has completed an internal NZQA-readiness mapping of 46 injection-moulding Book chapters to ten draft learning outcomes and selected current Injection Moulding standards. Before any recognised-assessment or micro-credential claim is developed, we want to validate the current standards, consent/moderation framework and most appropriate provider pathway.

Please help us clarify:

1. whether standards 252, 255, 27926, 29515, 260 and 9713 remain appropriate current anchors for an injection-moulding learning pathway;
2. how the live Injection Moulding domain's CMR 13 references should be applied alongside CMR 0014 version 15 for a new consent-to-assess application;
3. whether a provider-owned micro-credential, standards-based pathway, or another approach is the better first route;
4. which provider relationships should be explored given the 2026–2027 transition of work-based learning;
5. what evidence of employer/learner need and industry consultation would be most useful;
6. what pre-moderation, programme endorsement and national external moderation expectations a future provider should design for.

## Primary ask to Competenz

We want to understand the current operational plastics-training pathway before selecting a future provider model.

Please help us clarify:

1. the exact qualification/programme references and versions underlying current Level 3 plastics processing and Level 4 plastics engineering/injection-moulding pathways;
2. the current role of unit standards 252, 255, 27926, 29515, 260 and 9713;
3. how workplace observations, assessor judgements, verifier evidence and learner records are currently controlled;
4. whether MouldMaster's digital curriculum could be reviewed as a formative/support resource without being represented as recognised assessment;
5. which provider or transition route should be engaged for durable post-2027 delivery ownership.

## Primary ask to Plastics New Zealand

We are seeking industry-needs evidence, not endorsement.

Please help us identify:

1. the most important capability gaps for injection-moulding operators, setters, technicians and process engineers;
2. employers willing to review proposed learning outcomes;
3. employers willing to comment on practical evidence expectations;
4. potential pilot sites, only where the employer explicitly consents;
5. Māori, Pacific, disabled learner and other stakeholder perspectives that should be included in resource and pathway design.

## What MouldMaster can share at first contact

- 46-chapter curriculum map;
- ten draft learning outcomes;
- NZQA 2026 evidence matrix;
- current/expired standard inventory;
- provider assessment/moderation plan;
- evidence-template schemas;
- source register and claim boundaries;
- selected sample lessons and formative assessments.

Do not share learner PII, private employer records, proprietary process data, customer names, credentials, signing keys or confidential attachments.

## Non-negotiable claim boundary

Until supported by external written evidence, do not state that MouldMaster is NZQA approved, accredited, a qualification, nationally moderated, consented to assess, able to award NZQA credits, or evidence of workplace competence.
