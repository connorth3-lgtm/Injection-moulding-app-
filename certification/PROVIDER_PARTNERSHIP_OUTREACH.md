## Short introduction email

**Subject:** Injection moulding micro-credential / training partnership discussion

Hello,

I am developing **MouldMaster Academy**, a structured injection-moulding learning platform covering fundamentals, machine controls, materials, mould design, process setup, troubleshooting, scientific moulding, capability/statistics, automation and advanced process engineering.

The current programme includes a version-controlled curriculum, audited knowledge assessments, regional UK/US/NZ safety references, realistic troubleshooting cases and a practical supervisor-sign-off framework. The current certificates are explicitly non-accredited learning records.

I am looking for an NZQA-recognised provider interested in evaluating a partnership to develop a formally approved injection-moulding micro-credential or equivalent recognised training pathway. I would like to discuss:

- industry/learner need and appropriate target cohort
- NZQF level and credit mapping
- learning-outcome and assessment design
- practical workplace evidence
- assessor/moderator requirements
- moderation and learner-record systems
- pilot delivery and employer participation
- ownership/licensing/commercial arrangements

I can provide the curriculum, assessment architecture, quality manual, proposed micro-credential outcomes and technical source framework for review.

## Questions for a prospective provider
- Does injection moulding fit your current scope, industry partnerships and strategic priorities?
- Would you prefer a new micro-credential, an existing programme/module, or employer-specific training first?
- What evidence would you require before committing to programme development?
- Which staff would own academic/assessment approval and moderation?
- What NZQF level/credit range appears plausible after outcome mapping?
- What workplace evidence and assessor competence would you require?
- Can delivery include a self-paced digital component plus workplace assessment?
- What learner identity/record systems would be required?
- What commercial/licensing model would you consider?
- What pilot cohort size would you expect before formal submission?

## Pilot employer outreach

**Subject:** Industry input — injection moulding training pilot

Hello,

We are developing a structured injection-moulding training pathway and are seeking employer input on the skills that operators, setters and technicians most need to develop.

The proposed programme includes process fundamentals, material behaviour, defect troubleshooting, controlled process studies, safety awareness, process evidence, capability/statistics and practical workplace sign-off.

We are looking for employers willing to provide one or more of the following:
- a short interview on skills gaps
- review of proposed learning outcomes
- feedback on practical assessment tasks
- participation in a small pilot cohort
- post-training supervisor feedback

Participation would not be described publicly as an endorsement without written permission.
