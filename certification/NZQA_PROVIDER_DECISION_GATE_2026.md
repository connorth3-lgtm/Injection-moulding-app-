# NZQA provider decision gate — 2026

Status: **HOLD until external evidence exists**.

Do not select or describe a provider as MouldMaster's NZQA partner until all of the following are evidenced:

- the organisation's current NZQA registration/accreditation position is verified for the proposed pathway;
- the organisation confirms in writing that the proposed subject area and delivery model fit its scope and strategy;
- an accountable provider representative is identified for academic/assessment/provider decisions;
- the intended application owner is explicit;
- the provider confirms whether the route is a micro-credential, programme/qualification pathway, standards-based assessment, or another recognised form;
- final learning outcomes, NZQCF level, credits/notional learning hours and admission/completion rules are provider-controlled;
- assessment, reassessment, assessor capability, internal moderation, evidence retention, learner support, complaints/appeals and result reporting responsibilities are allocated;
- for DASS standards, current consent-to-assess and CMR requirements are confirmed with MAEISB/NZQA;
- workplace practical evidence arrangements, safety responsibilities and employer/site agreements are defined;
- commercial/IP/licensing terms do not weaken evidence, moderation, privacy or claim boundaries.
