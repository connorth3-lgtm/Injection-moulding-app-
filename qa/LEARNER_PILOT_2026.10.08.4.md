# MouldMaster real-learner pilot — 2026.10.08.4

This is the execution protocol for `data/learner-pilot-v1.json` and web release `2026.10.08.4`. The pilot remains exploratory: use real voluntary learners, keep baseline/immediate/delayed outcomes separate, include Book-boundary and diagnostic-transfer tasks, and retain only public-safe aggregate evidence.

For this release, the Virtual Apprenticeship and Spatial Twin may be used as diagnostic-transfer observation surfaces, but its automated reasoning score is not learner-outcome validation and must not be substituted for real-participant evidence.

Synthetic, AI-generated or developer-run results do not count as efficacy or psychometric evidence. Learner outcomes remain **HOLD**.
