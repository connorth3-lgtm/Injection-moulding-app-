## Findings

### P1 — Pressure definition

The original learner opening equated pressure with pushing force. That is inaccurate: pressure is force divided by area. The proposed canonical and served text now says this directly and keeps the fingertip/flat-palm example as an intuition aid. NIST’s SI guide supports the definition and unit relationship. Bind that source to the Book’s claim evidence record before any claim is promoted to “verified.”

## 50-source bibliography

The first 49 entries below are drawn from the Book’s evidence catalogue after deduplication and scope curation. Entry 50 is the NIST SI guide recommended for the pressure definition. Standards are linked to official landing pages; consult the applicable full text and edition before using a normative requirement. Manufacturer sources are scoped to the named equipment, material or system.

### Standards and measurement

1. **Standard Practice for Injection Molding Test Specimens of Thermoplastic Molding and Extrusion Materials** — ASTM International. [Source](https://store.astm.org/d3641-24.html). General test-specimen moulding practice; applicable material specification or supplier guidance controls recommended conditions.
2. **Standard Test Method of Measuring Shrinkage from Mold Dimensions of Thermoplastics** — ASTM International. [Source](https://store.astm.org/d0955-21.html). Thermoplastic moulding shrinkage test method and limitations of extrapolating standard specimens to production parts.
3. **Tools for moulding — Components of compression and injection moulds and diecasting dies — List of equivalent terms and symbols** — ISO. [Source](https://www.iso.org/standard/75669.html). Mould-component terminology and symbols.
4. **Plastics and rubber machines — Injection moulding machines — Safety requirements** — ISO. [Source](https://www.iso.org/standard/68000.html). Injection-moulding-machine safety requirements and safe-use information within scope.
5. **Plastics moulded parts — Tolerances and acceptance conditions** — ISO. [Source](https://www.iso.org/standard/90304.html). Current plastics-specific geometrical/dimensional tolerance and acceptance framework.
6. **Plastics — Injection moulding of test specimens of thermoplastic materials — Part 1** — ISO. [Source](https://www.iso.org/standard/67036.html). General injection-moulding principles, parameter descriptions and reproducible reporting; material-specific conditions vary.
7. **Plastics — Injection moulding of test specimens — Part 4: Determination of moulding shrinkage** — ISO. [Source](https://www.iso.org/standard/70413.html). Directional moulding and post-moulding shrinkage of thermoplastic test specimens.
8. **NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook, Chapter 2: Measurement Process Characterization** — NIST. [Source](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nistsematech-engineering-statistics-handbook-chapter-2-measurement-process). Repeatability, reproducibility, stability, calibration and uncertainty of measurement processes.
9. **NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook** — NIST. [Source](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/nistsematech-engineering-statistics-handbook). Engineering statistical methods including measurement characterization, process characterization, experimental design and monitoring.
10. **Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI), NIST Special Publication 811 (2008)** — National Institute of Standards and Technology. [Source](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/SP/nistspecialpublication811e2008.pdf). Defines the SI quantity relationship for pressure and explains that pressure is force divided by area.

### Peer-reviewed polymer and process research

11. **From Manual to Automated: Exploring the Evolution of Switchover Methods in Injection Molding Processes—A Review** — Polymers. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym17081096). Review of filling-to-holding switchover methods; supports early/late switchover consequences and sensitivity to material, machine and environmental conditions without defining a universal transfer point.
12. **A simple method of improving warpage and cooling time of injection molded parts simultaneously** — The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-022-09925-3). Experimental study comparing cooling-system configurations and their effects on cooling time and deformation in the studied injection-moulding setup; numerical results are study-specific.
13. **A review of research progress on the minimization of weld lines in injection molding** — The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-024-13607-7). Review of weld-line formation, influencing factors, mechanisms, prediction and improvement methods; does not establish a universal fix.
14. **Assessment of fiber orientation models predictability by comparison with X-ray µCT data in injection-molded short glass fiber-reinforced polyamide** — The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-024-12990-5). Experimental evidence that local fibre orientation in short-glass-fibre mouldings varies with flow and affects anisotropic properties and shrinkage/warpage-related behaviour.
15. **Multiple In-Mold Sensors for Quality and Process Control in Injection Molding** — Sensors. [Source](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23031735). Experimental multi-cavity pressure-sensor work covering clamping, switchover, holding and gate freeze-off interpretation.
16. **Recent progress in minimizing the warpage and shrinkage deformations by the optimization of process parameters in plastic injection molding: a review** — The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-022-08859-0). Multi-variable warpage/shrinkage mechanisms and process-parameter interactions; not a universal recipe.

### Process and quality methods

Used as process-control evidence, not as a universal pressure-setting source.
