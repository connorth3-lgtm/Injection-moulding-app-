# MouldMaster real-learner pilot — 2026.10.07.3

This is the execution protocol for `data/learner-pilot-v1.json` and web release `2026.10.07.3`.

The pilot remains exploratory. Use real voluntary adult learners, keep baseline/immediate/delayed outcomes separate, include Book-boundary and diagnostic-transfer tasks, and retain only public-safe aggregate evidence. Synthetic datasets, AI-generated responses and developer test accounts do not count.

The pilot may guide product iteration, but it does not establish population efficacy, psychometric validity, accreditation, production-recipe validity or machine-control authority. Learner outcomes remain **HOLD** until separately reviewed real longitudinal evidence satisfies the governed contract.
