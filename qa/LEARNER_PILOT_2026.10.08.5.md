# MouldMaster real-learner pilot — 2026.10.08.5

This is the execution protocol for `data/learner-pilot-v1.json` and web release `2026.10.08.5`. The pilot remains exploratory: use real voluntary learners, keep baseline/immediate/delayed outcomes separate, include Book-boundary and diagnostic-transfer tasks, and retain only public-safe aggregate evidence.

For this release, Mission Control may be used to observe whether learners can retain context, move through the mission timeline, use command/search and maintain an evidence chain across Learn, Book, Materials, Mould Master, Virtual Apprenticeship and Spatial Twin. Those local interaction records and automated reasoning scores are not learner-outcome validation and must not be substituted for real-participant evidence.

Synthetic, AI-generated or developer-run results do not count as efficacy or psychometric evidence. Learner outcomes remain **HOLD**.
