# Regional education course packs

Sources checked 8 November 2026. The MouldMaster course packs support injection moulding study and give providers a starting point for curriculum and assessment review.

## Learner and provider access

In the app, open **More → Learning & progress → Standards & readiness**, choose a region, and expand **Course and assessment pack** on the relevant route. The study and evidence map links each group to Book modules, app lessons and workbooks. The workbooks contain fictional cases and answers for self-checking.

In the Book, open **Book contents → Reading guide & editorial notes → Course workbooks and practice**. Read the workbooks there or open the matching course pack. After a complete web app install, these learning materials can also open offline. Official source links require a connection.

Under **Provider review and blank records**, download the draft assessor pack. It includes the map, observation guides where available, workbooks with feedback, source links, and blank delivery, assessment, moderation and pilot records. Keep real learner and workplace evidence in the provider's controlled system.

## India delivery hours

| Category | Hours |
| --- | ---: |
| Theory | 180 |
| Employability | 30 |
| Practical | 360 |
| Mandatory on-the-job training | 30 |
| Total | 600 |

The qualification file shows a classroom subtotal of 210 hours for theory and employability together. CIPET and its approved delivery arrangements control attendance, supervised practice, placement hours, and theory and practical assessment. The file gives aggregate minimums of 50% for theory and 70% for practical. The module summary shows 200 theory and 400 practical marks, while the annex shows 260 and 340. The app records the discrepancy and uses the centre’s controlled assessment instrument as the decision point. Four workbooks have draft Hindi study notes and self-checks for 5S, digital records, employability and enterprise. The centre and a vocational language reviewer check their suitability before formal use.

## Provider review and evidence

1. Confirm the programme, intake and any unit choices against the current official documents.
2. Review every mapping and supply the syllabus and assessment work marked for the provider. Agree the instruments, assessment language, evidence rules and reasonable adjustments.
3. Arrange authorised assessors, supervision and qualifying workplace opportunities. Confirm site procedures before work begins.
4. Review the materials with a subject specialist and run a learner and accessibility pilot. Record corrections and a moderation decision in the provider's system.

Providers control formal training hours, assessment, credit and qualification awards. The draft templates support planning and review. Site authorisations and procedures govern practical work.
