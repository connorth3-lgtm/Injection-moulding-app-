# Regional education pathways: NZ, UK, and India

Checked 8 November 2026. This note records the sources and scope used by the app's regional study pathways.

## What the mapping does

Open **More → Learning & progress → Standards & readiness** in the app or **Book → Reading guide & editorial notes → Regional study pathways**. Choose New Zealand, the UK or India, then open mapped Book modules or the app's Practice area. The regional view also lists activities for preparing fictional records and discussing the evidence a provider or workplace would need.

The crosswalk is provisional editorial study support. It does not claim formal equivalence, qualification approval, credit, provider consent to assess, qualification completion, workplace competence, or machine-operation authority. App activities and reading time do not count as mandated training or on-the-job hours. Independent curriculum and subject-matter review remains a separate gate.

## Framework coverage

| Region | Current source used | Mapping scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Zealand | NZQA qualifications 4401 and 4402, New Zealand Certificates in Plastics Processing at Levels 3 and 4 | Level 3 covers operator study for the optional Injection Moulding strand. Level 4 covers technician study in the Injection Moulding strand, including quality assurance, fault diagnosis and improvement. The existing NZQA readiness information remains available in the app. |
| England | Skills England ST1328 Polymer processing technician, Level 3, approved for delivery | Polymer-processing standard includes injection moulding among process technologies. The source states a typical 36-month duration, 3-month assessment period, and 626 minimum hours for compliance. Apprenticeship delivery and EPA remain external. |
| Scotland | Scientific, Technical and Formulation Processing Modern Apprenticeship, SCQF Levels 5 and 6 (SVQ codes GP42 22 and GP43 23) | Covers transferable processing skills. The approved centre confirms which units and workplace evidence apply to the learner's role. |
| Wales | Polymer Processing Operations workplace apprenticeships, Levels 2 and 3 | A Welsh provider lists both levels. Level 2 study covers safety, production, inspection and sampling. Level 3 study adds process diagnosis and improvement. The provider confirms the applicable framework issue, unit choices and intake. |
| Northern Ireland | Polymer processing operations Level 3 framework No. 348, Issue 6 (August 2025) | Polymer-processing framework requiring the specified competence and knowledge qualifications, essential skills, employment, and external workplace assessment. |
| India | CIPET Machine Operator – Injection Molding (MO-IM), NQR code QG-3.5-CP-04117-2025-V2-CIPET, Level 3.5 | Qualification file dated 26 May 2025; next review 25 May 2028. The file specifies 600 hours: 210 theory, 360 practical, and 30 mandatory OJT. App/Book activities do not satisfy those hours. |

## India module list from the NQR file

- CPC/N0214: process basics and job requirements — 90 hours.
- CPC/N0221: injection moulding operations, parameter monitoring, and troubleshooting — 270 hours.
- CPC/N0222: finished-product quality checks and inspection — 90 hours.
- CPC/N0411: workplace health and safety and 5S — 30 hours.
- CPC/N0223: entrepreneurship in injection moulding — 30 hours.
- CPC/N0219: MS Office/open-source office-suite basics — 30 hours.
- DGT/VSQ/N0101: employability skills — 30 hours.
- Mandatory OJT — 30 hours.

The NQR file lists Hindi as an additional language for the qualification and model curriculum. MouldMaster's interface and Book remain English.

## Mapping method and limits

- Map framework themes to Book module identifiers already present in the governed Book crosswalk.
- Offer fictional, formative worksheets for process records, inspection planning, safe work/5S, defect reasoning, and shift handover.
- Link to the official source used for each pathway so learners and providers can check current requirements.
- Keep provider, workplace, awarding-body, and regulator decisions outside MouldMaster.
- Recheck framework status and source details before any formal curriculum proposal or assessment design.
- Obtain independent human curriculum and injection-moulding SME review before describing the crosswalk as validated.
