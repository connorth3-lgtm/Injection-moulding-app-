# MouldMaster Engineering Calculation Contract

Version: 2026-09-02.1

## Core rule

The engine calculates only quantities supported by its inputs. It must never turn a generic assumption into a verified production value merely because the arithmetic is exact.

Every engineering value should retain an origin/confidence class: `MEASURED`, `OEM_SPEC`, `MATERIAL_SUPPLIER`, `CAD`, `CAE_SIMULATION`, `CALCULATED`, `EMPIRICAL`, `ESTIMATED`, or `GENERIC`. A result inherits the weakest critical input.

Machine suitability states are: `PASS_VERIFIED`, `PASS_PROVISIONAL`, `WARNING`, `FAIL`, and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`.

## Pressure domains

`HYDRAULIC`, `SPECIFIC_PLASTIC`, and `CAVITY` are separate domains. Hydraulic ↔ specific-plastic conversion is allowed only with a machine relationship. Cavity pressure must not be inferred from intensification ratio. Clamp-force calculations require effective cavity pressure or an explicitly labelled empirical estimate.

## Calculation contracts

### CALC-AREA-001 — Projected area

`A_total = cavities × A_part + A_cold_runner + A_other_pressurised`

Use projected silhouette along the clamp axis. CAD union area is preferred over a bounding rectangle.

### CALC-CLAMP-001 — Required clamp force

`F_kN = A_mm2 × P_cavity_MPa × reserve / 1000`

`1 MPa = 1 N/mm²`. Do not accept hydraulic or specific-plastic pressure as cavity pressure. Report kN and explicitly named tonne-force units rather than ambiguous “tons”.

### CALC-SHOT-001 — Recurring shot

Recurring shot = cavities × part + cold runner/sprue. Hot-runner manifold/drop inventory is not fully ejected each cycle and belongs in residence-time inventory, not recurring-shot volume.

### CALC-STROKE-001 — Screw stroke and cushion

`A_s = πD²/4`; `L_injection = V/A_s`; `L_metering = L_injection + target_cushion`.

Cushion is a screw-position/material reserve, not a percentage added to part volume.

### CALC-PRESSURE-001 — Pressure conversion

Hydraulic ↔ specific-plastic only, preferably using OEM/calibrated machine data. Cavity pressure cannot be generated by this conversion.

### CALC-FLOW-001 — Volumetric fill rate

`Q = V_fill / t_fill`; where screw geometry is known, `v_screw = Q/A_s`.

Prefer transferable volumetric flow/screw velocity over machine-specific “% speed”.

### CALC-PLASTICISING-001 — Throughput

`required kg/h = shot_g × 3.6 / cycle_s`.

Shot capacity alone is not a plasticising-capacity check.

### CALC-RESIDENCE-001 — Mean residence time

`mean residence = heated material inventory / mass throughput`.

Do not substitute advertised maximum shot weight for calibrated barrel inventory. Results are mean estimates; the actual material has a residence-time distribution. Hot-runner inventory can be included separately in the total heated system.

### CALC-COOL-001 — Theoretical cooling

`tc = s²/(π²α) × ln[(4/π) × (Tm-Tw)/(Te-Tw)]`

Require `Tm > Te > Tw`. This is a first-order one-dimensional conduction estimate and must be labelled theoretical. Use governing thick section, not an average wall, when that section controls ejection.

### CALC-CYCLE-001 — Event timeline

`cycle = close + fill + max(cooling_from_end_fill, hold + recovery) + open + eject + handling + other`.

This prevents hold/recovery/cooling overlap from being double-counted.

### CALC-SHRINK-001 — Tooling starting estimate

ISO-style moulding shrinkage: `L_mould = L_target/(1-S)`. A tooling-allowance definition is mathematically different and must be explicitly selected. Flow/transverse shrinkage must remain separate where the material is anisotropic. The result is never steel-cut authority by itself.

### CALC-CAVITIES-001 — Candidate cavity count

Candidate maximum = minimum of supplied constraints: shot, clamp, flow, plasticising, mould fit, and other validated constraints. A partial result must be labelled provisional.

### CALC-SUITABILITY-001 — Machine suitability

The overall machine result is the worst critical state across clamp, shot/stroke, pressure, flow, plasticising/recovery, residence, mould/tie-bar/platen fit, opening/daylight, ejection, temperature and auxiliary capability where those checks are available. Missing critical inputs produce `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, never an automatic pass.

## Regression vectors

- 27,000 mm² × 40 MPa × 1.20 = **1,296 kN**.
- 100 cm³ with Ø40 mm screw = **79.577 mm injection stroke**; +4 mm cushion = **83.577 mm**.
- 120 g shot / 24 s cycle = **18 kg/h**.
- 450 g heated inventory, 90 g shot, 30 s cycle = **2.5 min mean barrel residence**.
- 3 mm wall, α=0.10 mm²/s, 230/50/90 °C melt/mould/eject = **15.918 s theoretical cooling**.
- 100 mm target at 1.5% ISO-style shrinkage = **101.523 mm starting mould dimension**.
