**Subject:** Help us test MouldMaster Academy (non-production preview)

Hello,

We're inviting a small group of people to try **MouldMaster Academy**, an injection-moulding learning app, and tell us what is confusing, missing or difficult to use.

This is an **early, non-production learning preview**, not accredited training, a validated manufacturing tool or a source of production settings.

**Web release prepared for this invitation: `2026.10.09.6`**. The inviter must confirm this same release is actually deployed before sharing the link. It does not authorise changes to equipment or site procedures.

**Preview link (only after it has been verified by the inviter):**  
https://connorth3-lgtm.github.io/Injection-moulding-app-/preview/

A short [tester quick-start guide](https://github.com/connorth3-lgtm/Injection-moulding-app-/blob/preview/docs/TESTER_QUICK_START.md) explains the suggested route. About 15–20 minutes of your feedback is useful; there is no need to complete every module.

Please use a made-up learner profile and fictional training examples. Do not enter or send personal details, production data, confidential machine settings or customer information. Progress is stored locally in your browser/device, not in an online account. Read the [privacy notice](https://connorth3-lgtm.github.io/Injection-moulding-app-/preview/privacy.html) before use. If you see a broken page or no **Non-production preview** notice, stop and tell us.

Tell us which device/browser you used, what you expected and what happened. Report non-sensitive issues through [Report a learner problem](https://github.com/connorth3-lgtm/Injection-moulding-app-/issues/new?template=learner-problem.yml), or reply privately to the person who invited you with a **sanitised** description. The public GitHub form may require an account. Do not post screenshots, backups or sensitive data publicly.

Participation in this feedback exercise is voluntary. It is **not** a formal learner study, accreditation review, physical-device release validation, or assessment of professional competence.

Thank you for helping us make the learning experience clearer.

— MouldMaster Academy team
